  Why a separate process group alone isn't enough

  The cause. On Windows, Ctrl+C goes to every process on the console. django-q2
  makes only its sentinel ignore it (cluster.py:175). The pusher, monitor and
  worker processes are started fresh by spawn and use the default handler. They
  die with KeyboardInterrupt, and the sentinel's guard restarts them. That
  produces your "reincarnated … after sudden death" lines. The qcluster main
  process also gets the Ctrl+C, which is the path that stops the cluster
  cleanly.

  What CREATE_NEW_PROCESS_GROUP would do. It would stop Ctrl+C from reaching the
  worker and its children. But the only clean stop in django-q2 is in-process:
  Cluster.sig_handler, wired to SIGINT and SIGTERM only.
  - run.py could not trigger it from outside. On Windows, terminate() is a hard
    kill.
  - CTRL_BREAK_EVENT goes to the whole group, so it kills the sentinel, the
    pusher, the monitor and the worker at the same moment. That is abrupt, and
    it is not what the examiner should see either.
  - Doing it properly needs a custom worker entry point plus a stop-file
    protocol between run.py and the worker. That is much more code in the
    shutdown path, which currently has four passing Windows tests.
