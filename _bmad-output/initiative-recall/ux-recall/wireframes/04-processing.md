## Notes
- Real steps only. No percentage bar, no countdown, no spinning animation.
- Each status change is announced to screen readers ("Finding the key points, in progress").
- No partial summary is ever shown (FR-5).
- For 31 to 50 slides the time text reads "up to 2 minutes".
