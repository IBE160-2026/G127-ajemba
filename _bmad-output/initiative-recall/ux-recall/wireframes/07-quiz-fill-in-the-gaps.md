## Notes
- Matching rules follow FR-15: case and spaces ignored, one wrong letter accepted for words of 5 letters or more, short words must match exactly.
- The text field is not cleared while typing; Enter checks the answer.
- Same state, tab and progress rules as multiple choice (06).
- "Skip question" counts as not answered and adds the question to the review list (same rule as multiple choice). "Check answer" is disabled with the text "Type an answer first." until the field has text.
- The field has `spellcheck="false"` and `autocomplete="off"`.
